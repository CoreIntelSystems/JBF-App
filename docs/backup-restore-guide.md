# Backup and restore guide

All data lives on the phone, encrypted. If the phone is lost, stolen or
broken, the only way to get the records back is a backup kept somewhere
else. Only users with the backup permission (Super Administrator, General
Secretary, Treasurer) can do this.

## Automatic copies

Once a day, when the app opens, it keeps a safety copy on the phone. The
last 7 are kept and listed under **More > Backup and restore**. They help
if a mistake needs undoing, but **not** if the phone is lost.

## Make a backup

1. Open **More > Backup and restore**.
2. Tap **Create backup and share**.
3. Choose a password of at least 6 characters. Write it down and keep it
   safe: without it the backup cannot be opened, and nobody can recover it.
4. Share the `.jbf` file to somewhere off the phone: Google Drive, email
   to yourself, or another officer's phone.

Do this at least once a month, after every big collection day, and before
changing phones. Keep more than one recent backup.

## Restore on a new phone

1. Install the app (see the [installation guide](installation-guide.md)).
2. On the first screen tap **New phone? Restore from a backup**.
3. Choose the `.jbf` file and enter its password.
4. Check the summary (organisation, number of members and payments, last
   activity), confirm, and open the app again when it closes.
5. Sign in with the same username and password as on the old phone.

## Restore on a phone already in use

**More > Backup and restore > Restore from a backup file** replaces
everything on this phone with the backup. Anything recorded after the
backup was made is lost, so only do this when you are sure. The app keeps
the replaced database next to the new one until the next restore, just in
case.

## Restoring an automatic copy

Under **Automatic copies on this phone**, tap **Restore** next to a copy
to go back to that day. The same warning applies: later records are lost.

## Good practice

- Two officers should each hold a recent backup.
- Never send the backup password in the same message as the file.
- After restoring on a field phone, give it its own receipt prefix in
  **More > Organisation settings**.
