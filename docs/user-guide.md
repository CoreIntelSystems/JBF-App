# User guide

This guide is for officers who use the app day to day: secretaries,
treasurers, organisers, welfare and zone officers. What you see depends on
your role; if a button described here is missing, your role does not
include it.

The app has four tabs at the bottom: **Dashboard**, **Members**,
**Payments** and **More**.

## Signing in

Sign in with your username and password, or your PIN, or your fingerprint
once you have turned it on under **More > My login and security**. After
five wrong attempts the account is locked for five minutes.

## Dashboard

Shows members by status and zone, money collected today and this month,
dues outstanding and the number of defaulters, the latest payments and
recent registrations. Zone officers see figures for their own zone(s) only.

## Members

- **Add member:** tap **Add member**, fill in the form and choose 1 or 2
  zones (one is the main zone). The member ID (for example `JBF-000123`) is
  given automatically.
- **Find a member:** search by name, phone or member ID; filter by zone or
  status.
- **Member page:** contact details, zones, dues balance and payment
  history, welfare cases, positions held. From here you can record a
  payment, change status (active, inactive, suspended, deceased) or print
  an ID card.
- **ID cards:** on the Members tab, the **ID cards for this list** button prints cards for
  the members currently listed (10 per A4 page). Each card has a QR code
  used for attendance.

## Payments

- **Record payment:** choose the member, what it is for (for example
  Monthly welfare dues), the month paid for, the amount and the method
  (cash, mobile money or bank). Saving gives a receipt number such as
  `RCP-2026-000045`. Share the receipt as a PDF on WhatsApp or print it.
- **Meeting collection:** the group icon at the top of the Payments tab. Choose
  the contribution and month, enter the amount each, tick everyone who
  paid (tap an amount to change it for one person), then save all receipts
  at once.
- **Mistakes:** payments are never edited or deleted. Open the payment and
  tap **Void receipt**, giving a reason, then record it again correctly.
- **Defaulters:** the list of members who owe dues, largest balance first.
  Tap the chat icon next to a name to send a polite WhatsApp reminder, or
  the share button to send the whole list to a group.

## Welfare (More > Welfare)

1. A welfare officer reports a case: the member, the type of case (for
   example Hospitalisation) and what happened. The fixed benefit amount for
   that type is filled in.
2. The Welfare Chairman approves or rejects it. When the Chairman is away
   the General Secretary can approve; this is labelled on the case.
3. The Treasurer records the payout once the money is paid.

## Expenses and reports

- **More > Expenses:** record money spent with a category, who was paid and
  how. Each gets a voucher number. Mistakes are voided, not deleted.
- **More > Financial reports:** choose daily, weekly, monthly or annual and
  move between periods with the arrows. Export as PDF, Excel or CSV.
- **More > Charts:** monthly collections, zone comparison, welfare and
  member growth.

## Events and meetings (More > Events and meetings)

- **Events:** create an event with date, venue, budget and transport and
  accommodation plans. From the event page, **Collect money** records
  contributions or a silver collection linked to the event, and **Record
  expense** records spending for it. The page shows collected, spent and
  budget left.
- **Meetings:** general, executive, zone or welfare team meetings with an
  agenda and minutes.
- **Attendance:** open an event or meeting and tap **Take attendance**.
  Mark each person present, absent or excused, or tap **Scan ID cards** and
  point the camera at member cards. You can also type a member ID. When
  you are done, the top-right button marks everyone not yet marked as
  absent.
- You get a reminder on your phone the evening before each event or
  meeting you create.

## Executives (More > Executives)

Lists current office holders and past terms. Tap a person to see their
term and how many executive meetings they attended. The General Secretary
can appoint executives and end terms.

## Field phones (More > Field phones)

If you collect money on your own phone (for example as a zone treasurer),
send your collections to the main phone regularly:

1. Give your phone a name, keep **Only payments since the last send** on.
2. Tap **Send collections**, choose a password and share the file to the
   treasurer by WhatsApp or Bluetooth. Tell them the password separately.

## Reminders (More > Reminders)

Turn on the monthly dues reminder and pick the day of the month.
