Delete your account

June Borns Family (JBF) app and member website, operated by CoreIntel Systems for JBF. Last updated 19 October 2026.

How to ask

  1. Email info@coreintelsystems.org with the subject "Delete my JBF account". Send it from the phone number or email on your member record, and include your member ID (it looks like JBF-000123) and your full name.
  2. Or tell your Zone Secretary, who will pass your request on.
  3. We confirm it is you, then act on the request within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and password, so you can no longer use the app or website.
  • Your profile photo, phone number, email, address and other personal details.
  • Your chat messages and any announcements you posted are removed or made anonymous.

What is kept

  • Payment and receipt records, without your personal details, where the law or the association's accounts require them.
  • Election records are kept anonymously. Ballots never show who voted for whom.

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data without closing your account. Use the same email. See our privacy policy for the full picture.
