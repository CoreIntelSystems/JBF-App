Privacy policy

June Borns Family app and member website. Last updated 19 October 2026.

This policy explains what the June Borns Family (JBF) app and member website collect, why, and your choices. The app is built and operated by CoreIntel Systems for JBF. We follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we keep

  • Your name, member number, phone number, username, zone and the date you joined.
  • Optional details you or your Zone Secretary add: second number, address, about me, emergency contact and a profile photo.
  • Your dues, payments, receipts, welfare cases, event attendance and any office or role you hold.
  • Messages and files you send in member chats, and announcements sent to you.
  • Election records: that you voted, never how you voted. Ballots are kept separately and secret.
  • Your sign-in password, stored only as a salted hash.

Why we use it

Only to run the association: keep the membership list, record contributions and welfare, show you your own statement, hold meetings, events and elections, and let members and officers communicate. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use it for tracking.

Who can see it

  • You see your own records.
  • Members see other members only in their own zones, with name, username and zone.
  • Officers see what their role needs, such as the Treasurer for payments or the Welfare Committee for welfare cases.
  • A small CoreIntel support team can reach the data to keep the service running and to restore backups.

Where it is stored

Records are held on officers' phones in an encrypted database and in a secured cloud database (Supabase, hosted in Europe, Ireland). The member website is served by Render. Daily cloud backups are kept for 7 days. We use no third-party analytics or advertising services.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take profile photos and scan membership QR codes.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: optional, checked on your phone only.
  • Notifications: reminders for dues, meetings and events.
  • Internet: to sync and to sign in online.

Your choices

  • Correct your details under Me, or ask your Zone Secretary.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and personal details, by emailing info@coreintelsystems.org from the phone number on your record, or by telling your Zone Secretary.
  • When you leave the association and ask us, we remove your profile photo and contact details. Payment and receipt records are kept without personal details where the law or the association's accounts require it.

Children

The app is for adult members. It is not aimed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: info@coreintelsystems.org.
